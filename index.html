ChorusModulate delayed voices to thicken the sound. Edit delay, depth, rate and mix.

Center Frequency · 40 Hz–18 kHzMove the center of the band-pass filter. The upper limit is reduced at lower processing sample rates.
Width · 0–100%Widen the pass band: 0% is narrow (Q 12), 100% is wide (Q 0.5). The filter remains active at every width.
Comb Delay · 0.5–100 msSet the delay that determines the spacing of comb resonances.
